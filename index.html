About the author

A journey shaped by questions.

Decoding
HukumQuestion · Explore · Understand

Decoding Hukum is the work of Dilpreet Singh Brar, a writer whose curiosity has led him from formal education and civil-service studies towards deeper questions about spirituality, existence and the nature of the Creator.

A graduate of Chandigarh Group of Colleges (CGC), Mohali, Dilpreet brings together reflection, storytelling and an interest in the wisdom preserved across spiritual traditions and history.

His book explores ideas about the Creator through short stories, philosophical questions and true events from Sikh history. It is an invitation to think beyond familiar assumptions, examine different perspectives and approach profound questions with humility and an open mind.

Decoding Hukum extends that invitation beyond the book — creating room for readers to ask, discuss and explore without fear of judgement.

The intention

Not to impose conclusions, but to encourage sincere questions and a deeper search for understanding.

One question. Many paths of inquiry.
“Who is the Creator — and what does it mean to understand the One behind all existence?”
Explore

Bring your questions. Leave room for discovery.

Explore ideas across philosophy, spirituality, ancient scriptures and history. These themes are starting points for reflection, not fixed answers or boundaries around what can be discussed.

01 / THE CREATOR

The nature of the One

Explore questions about the origin, presence and nature of the Creator, and the relationship between the source and existence.

02 / CONSCIOUSNESS

Awareness and the self

Reflect on consciousness, identity, perception and the mystery of being aware.

03 / WISDOM & SCRIPTURE

Ideas across traditions

Consider philosophical and spiritual insights found in ancient texts and diverse traditions.

04 / SIKH HISTORY

Lessons through history

Look at true events and personalities from Sikh history, and the questions their lives and choices invite us to consider.

05 / KARMA & DESTINY

Choice and consequence

Examine the relationship between action, consequence, destiny and free will.

06 / EXISTENCE & PURPOSE

Our place in the whole

Ask what gives life meaning and how we understand our place in the wider universe.
